13. Project Governance and Handover

Operating structure

Maintain accountable ownership of project management, mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, automation, procurement/budget, safety/quality, outreach and business development. Roles may be shared but decision authority should be explicit.

Document management

Store source files in a shared controlled repository with version naming, revision log, reviewers and change approval. Keep public presentation material distinct from confidential test and supplier records.

Continuity

Prepare onboarding notes, system diagrams, bill of materials, CAD and code repositories, vendor contacts, open risks, test evidence and next-step priorities so future students can continue development.

16. Risk Register

Battery fire or thermal runaway — Critical

Control through qualified handling, quarantine, cell screening, engineering safeguards, appropriate storage and emergency planning. Owner and residual risk: To assign.

Cell variability — High

Control through identification, acceptance testing, compatible-cell matching and traceability.

Pressure-contact failure — High

Control through force/resistance validation, environmental tests and end-of-line checks.

Robot collision or pinch hazard — High

Control through task-based risk assessment, guarding, interlocks, safe speeds and authorized access.

Noncompliance / liability — Critical

Control through early regulatory review, third-party advice, documentation and release gates.

Supply / business model uncertainty — Medium

Control through conservative forecasts, recycler agreements, pilot feedback and scenario analysis.

17. Open Decisions and Action Register

Engineering

Confirm cell chemistry and geometry; establish qualified cell criteria; freeze electrical architecture; select contact system; determine enclosure material; validate BOM and CAD revisions.

Automation

Inventory actual robot, PLC, sensors and fixtures; produce I/O list; define cell layout and gripping; draft sequence and fault handling; measure cycle time and yield.

Operations

Approve collection and disposal SOPs; confirm recycler agreements; develop incident response; establish traceability and records retention.

Commercial

Identify payer, kiosk host, rental rules, insurance and service model; estimate lifecycle cost and pilot budget.

Evidence

Collect survey methodology, competition confirmations, prototype photos, engineering reports, test results and source references.

Assembly test record

Build ID | CAD/PCB revisions | cell IDs | station/operator | date | electrical test results | visual result | deviations | release status.

Engineering change request

Change ID | problem | affected documents | risk impact | proposed revision | reviewer | approval | effective date.

Incident / near-miss record

Date | location | equipment | event | immediate controls | personnel notified | investigation | corrective action | closure.

Evidence and reference log

Claim | source document/link | author | date | verification status | approved external wording.

Appendix B. Glossary

BOM

Bill of Materials — controlled list of components, quantities and purchasing details.

PLC

Programmable Logic Controller — industrial controller coordinating station logic and signals.

HMI

Human–Machine Interface — controls and status display for operators.

Traceability

Ability to connect each finished assembly to component lots, inspections and tests.

Pressure-fit contact

Mechanical electrical connection established by controlled contact force rather than direct cell soldering.
