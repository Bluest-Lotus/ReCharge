11. Business and Deployment Model

Customers and beneficiaries

Students, educational institutions, campuses and other high-traffic facilities are potential beneficiaries. Proposed institutional payers and operating partners must be validated through interviews and pilots.

Concept

A powerbank lending/rental kiosk could support short-term charging needs. Potential revenue includes rental fees and advertising; kiosk displays may also support public-service or emergency messaging subject to governance and technical validation.

Economics

Build a cost model covering collection, qualified labor, cell testing/rejection, electronics, enclosure, automation, energy, quality assurance, certification, kiosk capital, maintenance, insurance, replacement and recycler fees. No break-even estimate is currently verified.

Pilot

Start with a supervised, limited-access pilot only after product safety review, operational permissions, user instructions, maintenance processes and incident response are in place.

12. Partnerships, Outreach and Achievements

Project history

ReCharge evolved from lithium recovery and awareness into the Phoenix Pack prototype and an automated manufacturing capstone. A collection drive and project showcases provided early traction.

Reported recognition

Prior project records mention an Innovation & Impact Champion recognition, second place in the Canadian Tire Environmental Sustainability Challenge, Ford Innovation Showcase involvement, Red Bull Basement 2026 Canada idea recognition, and a St. Clair College innovation prize. Verify official event names, dates and award wording before publication.

Partner needs

Potential partners include campus administration, student council, verified electronics/battery recyclers, engineering reviewers, insurers, kiosk host sites and manufacturing sponsors.

15. Financial and Procurement Controls

BOM fields

Part ID; description; revision; supplier; approved alternative; unit price; quantity; extended price; lead time; lifecycle status; and safety/quality notes.

Cost tracking

Distinguish research costs, prototype costs, pilot costs and recurring production costs. Track cell rejection losses and testing labor, not merely purchased component prices.

Funding

Document grants, prizes, sponsorships, in-kind equipment and restrictions. Maintain receipts and a dated budget-versus-actual register.
